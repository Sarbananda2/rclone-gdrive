Privacy policy

rclone Google Drive (personal) · Last updated 6 November 2026

Who uses this app

This Google OAuth client is used only by its owner, with rclone on the owner's own computer, to access the owner's own Google Drive. It has no other users.

What data it accesses

Files and folders in the owner's Google Drive, through the Google Drive API, and only while the owner runs rclone.

Where the data goes

Directly between Google's servers and the owner's computer. The app has no server of its own, and nothing is sent anywhere else.

What is stored

The sign-in token and a cache of recently opened files, both on the owner's computer only.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or given to anyone. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
