rclone Google Drive (personal)

This is a personal, single-user Google OAuth client for rclone, the open-source command-line program for cloud storage.

Its only user is its owner, who uses it to show their own Google Drive as a folder on their own Linux computer. It is not a public service, it has no other users, and there is nothing to sign up for.
